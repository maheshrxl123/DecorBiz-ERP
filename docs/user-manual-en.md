# DecorBiz User Manual (English)

## 1. Overview
DecorBiz is an ERP and Storefront system designed specifically for Tiles, Sanitaryware, Faucets & CP, and Construction Chemicals dealers.

## 2. Counter POS Billing
- **Item Search & Scan**: Type any item name in English or Hindi, SKU, or scan the product barcode using a USB barcode scanner or camera.
- **Tile Unit Conversions**: Enter tiles by Box, Piece, Sq Ft, or Sq M. The system automatically converts rates and coverage.
- **Discounts**: Cashiers can apply discounts up to the variant limit. Higher discounts require Manager approval.
- **Payments**: Accept Cash, UPI, Card, or Credit (Khata).
- **Invoices**: Prints in A4, A5, 58mm thermal, and 80mm thermal with GST breakdown, amount in words, and UPI QR code.

## 3. Tile Area Calculator
On any quotation or tile detail page, enter room Length (ft), Width (ft), and Wastage % (default 10%). The calculator computes exact square footage and rounds up to the required number of whole boxes.

## 4. Parties & Khata
Track customer ledgers, credit limits, payments, and print statement of accounts to share on WhatsApp.
